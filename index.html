Co-op Work Term Report

Lochlan Bridger

Software Developer Co-op at Tucker Professional Corporation

Summer 2026

01 — Abstract

Introduction

This site gives a brief overview of my Summer 2026 co-op term at Tucker Professional Corporation. Its goal is to illustrate my progress and accomplishments during the work term.

02 — The Company

About the Employer

Tucker Professional Corporation is an accounting firm with a focus on accounting and advisory services for medical professionals. Jonathan Tucker employed me as a full-stack developer to create a SaaS related to both accounting and the medical scene.

03 — Goals

Goals & Learning Outcomes

I set three goals for this term. The first was communication — learning to explain technical concepts, capabilities, and use cases to a non-technical audience, which I worked toward by discussing AI concepts directly with my non-technical employer and by hearing from my technical supervisor how he approaches non-technical clients. The second was building a working knowledge of the software development and accounting concepts this project needed, from agile practices and CI/CD to Azure resources and accounting for long-term care homes in Ontario. The third was staying organized and self-directed while developing a long-term project remotely and largely on my own, which I approached by using Jira to track tasks and by following agile practices.

I successfully conveyed the capabilities, costs, and use cases of the AI models we were building around, and came away with a better sense of how a person's role shapes what they need to know about a technical concept. I also built a solid foundation in Azure, agile development, CI/CD pipelines, database design, and the accounting practices specific to long-term care homes. On organization, I got more done than I expected and procrastinated far less than I thought I would while working alone.

04 — Job Description

The Work

My primary project was the solo development of a Minimum Viable Product for a software that ingests and processes financial data with support from AI models. The most interesting aspects of the job was learning about the development process. I became familiar with agile development, creating an Azure environment, documentation for future employees, and designing my own CI/CD pipeline. All of these concepts were new to me and I realized how impactful the development process has on the quality of work. I am also grateful for increasing my understanding in concepts needed for full-stack development, including database and API design.

05 — Conclusion

Conclusions

If there's one thing I'd want a reader to take from this report, it's that I learned the importance of good development practices and organization while working on a large project. I am also thrilled to have familiarized myself with basic knowledge required to develop a full-stack application.

06 — Thanks

Acknowledgments

Thank you to Fred Turner for their mentorship and patience throughout the term, and to Katie Grierson, my co-op coordinator, for their support before and during the placement.
